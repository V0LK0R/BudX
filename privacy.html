Privacy Policy for BudX

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Data Collection

BudX collects data necessary for your plant tracking and smart device management, including:

  • User account information (email).
  • Plant data you manually enter.
  • Device connection credentials (stored securely).

Data Usage

We use your data only to provide you with tracking, reminders, and smart device control services within the app. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time via Settings > Danger Zone in the BudX app. If you have trouble accessing this, email support@budx.info with your request to delete your account.
